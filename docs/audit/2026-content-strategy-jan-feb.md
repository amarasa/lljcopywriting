## Content Specifications

### Writing Guidelines

- **Voice:** Warm, knowledgeable, empathetic (matches Larissa's brand)
- **Reading level:** 8th grade (accessible to families and marketers)
- **No jargon** unless explained
- **Include examples** whenever possible
- **Personal anecdotes** where appropriate (Larissa's background)

### SEO Requirements Per Post

- [ ] Target keyword in title (front-loaded when natural)
- [ ] Target keyword in first 100 words
- [ ] 2-3 secondary keywords naturally included
- [ ] Meta description (150-160 characters) with keyword
- [ ] At least one internal link to services or other posts
- [ ] H2/H3 structure with keywords where natural
- [ ] Image alt text (when images added)

### Technical Setup Required

1. **Blog route** (/blog) - Sanity CMS integration
2. **Blog post template** with:
   - Author byline
   - Publish date
   - Category/tags
   - Reading time
   - Social sharing
   - Related posts
   - CTA section
3. **Sitemap update** to include blog posts
